Your trust matters.

This Privacy Policy applies to Fit the Blocks and to this website. By using the game or visiting these pages, you acknowledge the practices described here.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the game, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or support legitimate business and operational needs.

7. Children’s Privacy

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that should be reviewed or removed, please contact us and we will review the request in accordance with applicable law and platform requirements.
