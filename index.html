How it plays

  • Place incoming shapes on the 8×8 board with as little waste as possible.
  • Complete rows or columns to clear space and lift your score.
  • Use your limited shuffle wisely when the board stops giving you room.
  • Press Settle when your score is high enough and the timing feels right.

Why players stay

  • Clear visual feedback that makes each smart move feel earned.
  • Score targets and star goals that create strong short-term motivation.
  • A handcrafted level path that steadily deepens the challenge.
  • Relaxed controls paired with meaningful decisions every single round.
